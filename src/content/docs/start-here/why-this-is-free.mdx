I think knowledge should be free.

Plenty of this isn't, and there is no pretending otherwise. The aircraft, the
fuel, your instructor's hour — those cost what they cost. So does the kit:
headsets, books, a tool that does the arithmetic for you.

Understanding is not like those things. Explaining why the rotor RPM decayed
costs the same whether one person reads it or ten thousand do. A price on that
is a decision somebody made, not a cost somebody is covering. It is why
libraries exist, and it is the reason this site does too.

You are already paying for the expensive part. Understanding what happened in
the hour you just flew shouldn't be another line on the bill.

So there is nothing gated here. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no "unlock
the rest of this lesson". Every course, every subject area, every quiz and
every flashcard deck is open to anyone who turns up.

## What it costs to run

Almost nothing, and that is on purpose.

This site was built so that it does not get more expensive as more people read
it. Nothing behind it charges by the visitor, so there is no month where it
suddenly has to start paying for itself. That matters more than it sounds: a
free site with a bill attached eventually stops being free, and usually right
when it has become useful to people.

Your progress and quiz scores are saved on the device you are reading on,
rather than in an account. That is the trade for never asking you to make one —
and it is why your ticks don't follow you from a laptop to a phone.

## What it costs to write

Time. That is the part that is actually scarce.

A lesson is a couple of thousand words that have to be correct, in the right
order, and at a depth you can absorb at that point in your training — then
checked against the handbook, the regulation, or the flight manual it came
from. Getting that wrong in aviation material is not a typo.

I also build **[Flight Token](https://flighttokenapp.com/)**, a weight and
balance and performance app for helicopter pilots. If it's useful to you, a
subscription helps cover the time this site takes. That is the entire funding
model.

If it isn't useful to you, use the courses anyway. That's the point of them.

## How you can help instead

Nothing here needs your money. A few things are worth more than it, and the
first two reach me at **[matt@flighttokenapp.com](mailto:matt@flighttokenapp.com)**:

- **Tell me when something is wrong.** A mistake caught by a reader is the most
  valuable thing this site gets. You don't need to know how to fix it — just
  say what looked off, and where you saw it.
- **Write something, if you instruct.** If you already teach this material you
  have done the hard part. Get in touch and I'll help with the rest.
- **Point a student at it.** That costs nothing, and it is how anyone finds
  this.
