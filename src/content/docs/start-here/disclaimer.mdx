## Disclaimer

<Aside type="danger" title="This is study material, not authority">
Nothing on this site is a substitute for an authorized flight instructor, the
Rotorcraft Flight Manual or Pilot's Operating Handbook for the aircraft you are
flying, or the current Federal Aviation Regulations.
</Aside>

Specifically:

- **This is not an FAA-approved training course.** Reading it does not satisfy any
  training, ground instruction, or endorsement requirement. Only an appropriately
  rated and authorized instructor can provide and log those.
- **Do not use this material for flight planning or in-flight decisions.** Use
  current official sources.
- **Limitations, procedures, and performance numbers vary** by model, serial
  number, equipment, and manual revision. Where this site mentions a number, it is
  for illustration. The authoritative number for your aircraft is in your
  aircraft's flight manual.
- **Regulations change.** Where this site discusses a regulation, read the current
  regulation at [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/).
- **This material is written by volunteers and may contain errors.** Check the
  status badge on each page. If you find a mistake, please
  [report or fix it on GitHub](https://github.com/Masmith530/collective_knowledge/issues).
- **No warranty.** The content is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.
  You are responsible for your own decisions as a pilot.
